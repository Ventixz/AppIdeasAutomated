Minimum Spanning Tree

Give it a weighted graph and it finds the cheapest set of links that keeps everything connected — no cycles, nothing wasted. The chosen edges are the tree; the faded ones were dropped for being too heavy. Two classic algorithms — Kruskal's and Prim's — compute it, and the answer is double-checked for minimality.

Your weighted links

One link per line: a -- b : 7. Separators: -- - or a space (arrows are read as plain links — a tree has no direction). Weight markers: : = @ | or (7); no weight means cost 1. A single word is an isolated node. # starts a comment.
