Connected Graph

Take a graph as input and answer the one question in its name: is every node connected? The page doesn't just say yes or no — it shows why. It finds the single points of failure ( cut vertices and bridges) whose loss would break an undirected graph apart, and for a directed graph it separates weak from strong connectivity and colours the strongly-connected clusters.
