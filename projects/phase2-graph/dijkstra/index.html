Dijkstra's Algorithm

Give it a weighted graph and a place to start, and it finds the cheapest path to everywhere at once — not just the number, but the route. The source grows a shortest-path tree one node at a time, always settling the nearest unsettled node next; pick a target and the path lighting up is the answer, and it's double-checked against Bellman–Ford.
