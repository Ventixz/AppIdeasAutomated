Shape Area & Perimeter Classes

An abstract Shape with one subclass per figure — Circle, Rectangle/Square, Triangle (general, right & equilateral), Rhombus, RegularPolygon, Ellipse — each supplying only its own area() and perimeter(). The eighth task in karan/Projects' Classes category. The point is the design, not the buttons: nothing ever switches on a shape's type, it asks the object; measurements are exact values, not floats, so a circle's area is literally 49π and a 3-4-5 triangle's perimeter is exactly 12; and the design is honest about what can't be exact — a pentagon's area and an ellipse's perimeter are shown as approximations, flagged. The same classes run under Node (see the README); this page just drives them.

Compute a shape

Pick a shape, type its dimensions (whole numbers or fractions like 5/2 are fine — the arithmetic stays exact), and the object computes its own area and perimeter.
