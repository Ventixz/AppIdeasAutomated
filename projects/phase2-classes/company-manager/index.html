Company Manager

An abstract Employee and its subclasses — HourlyEmployee, SalariedEmployee, Manager and Executive — gathered into a Company that hires, fires and pays them. The third task in karan/Projects' Classes category. The point is the design, not the buttons: you cannot instantiate the abstract base, every subclass computes pay its own way (hourly with real weekly overtime, salary sliced into periods, a manager's bonus, an executive's profit share), and money is kept in integer cents so every total is exact. The same classes run under Node (see the README); this page just drives them.
