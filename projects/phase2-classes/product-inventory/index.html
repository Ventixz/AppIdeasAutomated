Product Inventory

A Product class (price, id, quantity) and an Inventory class that holds many products and sums their value — the first task in karan/Projects' Classes category. The point here is the design, not the buttons: money is kept as integer cents so totalling a catalogue is exact, and the objects enforce their own rules — you can't oversell stock, drive a quantity negative, or register two products under the same id. The same classes run under Node (see the README); this page just drives them with a live table.
