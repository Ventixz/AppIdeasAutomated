Bank Account Manager

An abstract Account and its subclasses — CheckingAccount, SavingsAccount and BusinessAccount — gathered into a Bank that opens them and moves money between them, ATM-style. The fourth task in karan/Projects' Classes category. The point is the design, not the buttons: you cannot instantiate the abstract base, every subclass debits its own way (checking overdraws to a limit then charges a one-time fee, savings never overdraws but earns interest and caps withdrawals, business pays a fee on every transaction), money is kept in integer cents and the balance is derived from the ledger so the statement can't lie, and every transfer is atomic. The same classes run under Node (see the README); this page just drives them.
