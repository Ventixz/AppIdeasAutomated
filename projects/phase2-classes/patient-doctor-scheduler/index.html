Patient / Doctor Scheduler

An abstract Person and its subclasses — Patient and Doctor — gathered into a Scheduler that books appointments and keeps them honest. The fifth task in karan/Projects' Classes category. The point is the design, not the buttons: you cannot instantiate the abstract base, time is a discrete slot grid (an 8-hour day is exactly 16 half-hour slots, parsed onto the grid — 09:15 is refused, not rounded), a doctor refuses a double-booking and more than 16 appointments a day, a patient can't be in two places at once, and every booking is atomic. A patient's appointments are derived from the doctors' schedules, so the two views can't disagree. The same classes run under Node (see the README); this page just drives them.
