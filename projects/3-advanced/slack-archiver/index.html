Slack Archiver

Slack's free tier purges messages past 10,000, taking a team's institutional knowledge with them. This archiver extracts channel history a rate-limited page at a time — resuming from the last message it saw — into a local database you can search and export. Only a Team owner can drive it.

Switch to a non-owner to see the owner-only controls lock — the spec requires that only Team owners may archive.
