Prime factorization

Enter a whole number and see it broken into its prime factors. Done in the browser with exact integer (BigInt) arithmetic, so the answer stays correct past 2⁵³, and with Pollard's rho + Miller–Rabin so even 18–20 digit numbers factor in a blink instead of hanging the tab.

Any integer ≥ 1. Big semiprimes are the slow case — 20-ish digits is comfortable; much larger may take a moment.
