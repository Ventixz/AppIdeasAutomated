Exact unit converter

Temperature, length, mass, volume, area, speed, time, digital storage and currency. Every conversion factor is a defined exact ratio (1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 mi = 1609.344 m), so it runs on BigInt fractions: 100 mi → km → mi lands back on exactly 100, where a float engine would drift. Results that terminate are shown in full; the rest are rounded and say so. Currency uses an editable offline snapshot and is always flagged approximate.
