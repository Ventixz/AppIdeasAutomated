Distance between two cities

The shortest path between two places on Earth is a great-circle arc, not the straight line you'd draw on a flat map. This computes it with the haversine formula (a sphere) and Vincenty's formula (the WGS-84 ellipsoid — Earth is not a sphere), reports the initial compass bearing, and converts into the unit you pick. Cities come from a small offline gazetteer, so there's no API key and nothing to install. You can also type raw coordinates.

—

Equirectangular projection (a lat/lon grid — no map tiles). The curved line is the great-circle path; on this projection the shortest route looks bent, which is exactly the point.
