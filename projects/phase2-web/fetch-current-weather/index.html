1 · Look up a place

There is no "weather for a place name" endpoint. A name is geocoded to coordinates first — and names are ambiguous, so you get a list to pick from. Then the coordinates are used to fetch the current conditions.

2 · Why the thermometer is the wrong number

A thermometer reads the air temperature. It is not what the air feels like: wind strips warmth off your skin (wind chill), and humidity stops sweat evaporating (heat index). Each formula only applies in its own domain — apply the wrong one and you get nonsense. Drag the sliders and watch which model takes over, and where the boundaries are.
