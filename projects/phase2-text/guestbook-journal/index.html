Guestbook / Journal

A place where people can leave a note — a guestbook, a journal, or a shout box. Whatever a visitor types is shown as text, never markup, so a stray <script> is harmless and a bare https://… becomes a safe link. Timestamps read like a human (just now, yesterday), entries are grouped by day, and the book can be open, moderated, or closed. Everything lives in your browser — no build step, no server, no network.
