Fizz Buzz

The classic interview warm-up — print 1 to 100, but say Fizz on multiples of 3, Buzz on multiples of 5, and FizzBuzz on multiples of both. Here it's turned into a playground: the rules are data, so you can change the divisors and words, pick any range, and watch the sequence rebuild live — with every line verified against an independent check.

The rules

0 rules

Each rule is a divisor and a word. A number is replaced by the words of every rule that divides it, joined in order — or kept as-is when none match. Classic Fizz Buzz is just 3 → Fizz and 5 → Buzz.
