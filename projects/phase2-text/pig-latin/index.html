Pig Latin

"Move the first letter to the end and add ay" is the version everyone remembers — and it's wrong. What actually moves is the onset, the run of consonants before the first vowel (smile → ilesmay), and "vowel" is slippery: y is a consonant at the start of a word but a vowel inside it, and qu travels as a unit.
