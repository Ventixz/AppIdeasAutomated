RSS Feed Creator

Paste the XML of an RSS 2.0 or Atom 1.0 feed and get all its posts, normalised into one shape no matter which vocabulary the feed speaks — two different date formats, two link conventions and the usual dc:/content: extensions all handled. The reader's writer is its inverse: rebuild a clean RSS 2.0 file from the posts and download it. Everything runs in your browser — no server, no network, no dependencies.
